📜 SERVER RULES

Play fair. Respect all. Have fun.

#1
Respect Everyone

Respect all players and server members, whether they are new or experienced.

#2
No Toxic Behaviour

Abusive, excessively offensive, or intentionally harmful behaviour in chat or voice chat is not allowed.

#3
No Harassment & Bullying

Do not repeatedly target, annoy, threaten, or mentally harass another player.

#4
No Griefing

Do not destroy, damage, or intentionally disrupt another player's house, farm, base, or builds without permission.

#5
No Stealing

Do not take items from another player's chest, shop, storage, or personal area without permission.

#6
Fair Gameplay

Hacks, X-ray, cheats, unfair modifications, or anything that provides an unfair advantage are not allowed.

#7
No Bug Exploiting

Do not abuse glitches or bugs to duplicate items, gain unfair advantages, bypass systems, or cheat.

#8
No Duplication Exploits

Do not intentionally duplicate items using glitches or exploits.

#9
No Combat Logging

Do not intentionally disconnect during combat to avoid consequences.

#10
No Unfair PvP

PvP must remain fair. Do not use exploits or unfair mechanics to gain an advantage.

#11
Respect Builds

Do not claim, destroy, modify, or interfere with another player's build without permission.

#12
No Advertising

Do not advertise unrelated servers, communities, or services without staff permission.

#13
Staff Decisions

Respect server staff decisions. If you disagree with a decision, contact staff calmly.

#14
Have Fun

The most important rule: enjoy the server, make friends, build amazing things, and have fun.
